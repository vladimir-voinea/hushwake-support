Hushwake

An alarm only you can hear. Hushwake plays your alarm through your headphones or earbuds — and only through them. If they disconnect, it wakes you with vibration instead. It never uses the speaker.

• Wake at a time, or nap for 10, 20, 26, 45 or 90 minutes
• Squeeze your earbuds or press the headphone button to stop
• Gentle rising chimes and an optional wind-down sound
• No ads, no accounts, no tracking, no network
