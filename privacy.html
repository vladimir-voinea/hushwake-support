Privacy Policy

Last updated: 8 November 2026

Hushwake does not collect, store, transmit or sell any personal data.

What stays on your device

Your settings and saved alarms are stored only on your iPhone or iPad. Hushwake reads which audio output is active (for example "AirPods Pro") only to decide whether it is safe to play sound; this is never stored or sent anywhere.

No network, no tracking

Hushwake has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no third-party SDKs. It does not connect to the internet.

Notifications

If you turn on the optional backup banner, Hushwake schedules a local notification on your device. Nothing leaves your device.

Children

Hushwake collects no data from anyone, including children.
